About this site

What this is

A list of every bill and resolution that Ohio State Representative Christine Cockley (House District 6) sponsors or cosponsors in the 136th General Assembly, with the current status of each and, where one has been reviewed, a plain-language summary.

Who built it

Volunteer students at The Ohio State University, working with Rep. Cockley's office. This is not an official website of the State of Ohio or of the Ohio House of Representatives. For official information, see ohiohouse.gov.

Where the data comes from

Which bills she sponsors or cosponsors, and the current version of each, come from her official page on ohiohouse.gov. Committee action and dates come from the Ohio Legislature's public legislative information service. Bill titles and status labels are reproduced verbatim from those sources; this site does not rewrite them. Every bill links to its official page at legislature.ohio.gov, which is authoritative if anything here disagrees with it.

The site is rebuilt from those sources every morning. If a bill's status is wrong here, it is almost always because this site has not rebuilt since the Legislature updated it.

How the summaries are written

Each plain-language summary is drafted from the Ohio Legislative Service Commission's neutral analysis of the bill, then checked by a second person against that analysis before it is published. Every published summary names its reviewer and the date of review. A summary that has not been through that review is not published at all — those bills show their official title only.

Summaries describe what a bill would do. They do not say whether a bill is good or bad, and they do not predict whether it will pass.

How often it updates

Automatically, once every morning. This page was last built on {{ built }}.

How to report an error

Email Rep. Cockley's office at rep06@ohiohouse.gov with the bill number and what is wrong. Corrections to a summary or a status can be made without waiting for the next scheduled update.
